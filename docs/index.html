naam-match

Is “आयुष देवांगन” the same person as “Aayush Devangan”? Compare names across Hindi, Bengali, Gurmukhi, Gujarati, Odia, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam and English. Every decision shows its working. Relations like “S/o” or “पिता” are checked too, so two people with the same name but different fathers are not merged. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

How it decides

Indian scripts are transliterated to Latin; honorifics (Shri, Smt., श्रीमती) are dropped and suffixes like Bai or Devi set aside; spelling variants are folded (aa→a, ee→i, w→v, sh→s, ou→au); word order is ignored; each word is scored with Jaro–Winkler. Match needs every word to agree strongly and no initials. Initials can only ever lead to Send for review. When both names give a father's or husband's name, those must match too before a pair is auto-matched. It is a transparent heuristic, not a trained model: check the review pile, and read the benchmark and its limits.
